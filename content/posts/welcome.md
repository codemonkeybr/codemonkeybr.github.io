I keep a `~/dev` folder full of projects — a mesh radio network, a NomadNet game server, home
automation, whatever I got curious about that month. This blog is where I write those projects
up: what I built, what actually worked, and what I'd do differently if I were starting over.

If you want a mental model for what to expect here, think of Stephen King's *Nightmares &
Dreamscapes* — a single collection made up of a dozen-plus stories that share nothing in
particular except that they came out of the same person's head. No shared universe, no continuing
plot, just whatever the author felt like writing that year, bound together because it was useful
to bind them together. That's this blog. One post might be a deep dive into Reticulum radio
networks; the next might be about flashing custom firmware onto some radio board, or a home
automation dashboard, or something I haven't even started yet. They won't have much to do with
each other, and that's fine — the throughline is just that I built it.

Some of it will come in short series, when a project has enough ground to cover that one post
would be a wall of text. Most of it won't. I'm not trying to build toward some grand unified
thing here — just writing down what I learned while it's still fresh, in case any of it is useful
to someone else doing something similar.
